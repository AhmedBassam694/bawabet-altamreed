Shock

Shock is a drop in blood circulation caused by an imbalance between blood volume and circulatory capacity, leading to inadequate oxygen supply to body tissues.

Types of Shock

  • Surgical Shock
  • Neurogenic Shock
  • Septic Shock

Stages of Shock

1. Reversible Shock

Responds to treatment with improvement in pulse, blood pressure and urine output.

2. Irreversible Shock

Does not respond to treatment and vital signs continue to deteriorate.

Surgical Shock

Causes

  • Massive blood loss
  • Burns
  • Vomiting
  • Severe diarrhea

Symptoms

  • Pallor
  • Profuse sweating
  • Rapid weak pulse
  • Low blood pressure
  • Rapid shallow breathing

Treatment

  • Lay patient flat with head lowered
  • Blood transfusion
  • IV fluids (Ringer's, Lactated Ringer's, Glucose Saline)
  • Vasopressors
  • Hydrocortisone
  • Treat the cause

Neurogenic Shock

Cause

  • Severe pain

Symptoms

  • Slow weak pulse
  • Low blood pressure
  • Pallor
  • Sweating

Treatment

  • Lay patient flat with head lowered
  • Stimulate the patient
  • IV fluids

Septic Shock

Causes

  • Severe infection
  • Pneumonia
  • Peritonitis
  • Internal abscess
  • Gangrenous wounds

Symptoms

  • High temperature
  • Rapid pulse
  • Low blood pressure
  • Local signs of infection

Treatment

  • Treat the cause
  • IV fluids
  • Blood & Plasma transfusion
  • Strong antibiotics
  • IV Zantac
  • Improve oxygenation
