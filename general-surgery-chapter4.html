Complications of Anesthesia

First: General Anesthesia

  • Malignant Hyperthermia
  • May appear during surgery or within 24 hours.
  • Increased pulse.
  • High blood pressure.
  • High temperature.
  • Muscle rigidity.
  • Nausea and vomiting.
  • Vocal cord spasm.
  • Urinary retention.
  • Hypothermia.
  • Rare nerve injury from wrong positioning.

Second: Spinal Anesthesia

  • Hypotension.
  • Respiratory disturbance.
  • Headache.
  • Urinary retention.
  • Meningitis (Rare).
  • Nerve injury (Rare).

Complications of Fluid Transfusion

Local Complications

Leakage Around Cannula

  • Swelling.
  • Pain.
  • Drip stops.
  • Move cannula.
  • Apply ointment.

Phlebitis

  • Swelling.
  • Bluish discoloration.
  • Pain.
  • Warm vein.
  • Stop IV infusion.
  • Warm compresses.
  • Anti-inflammatory ointment.

Cannula Infection

  • Microbial infection.
  • Prevent with sterilization.

General Complications

1- Pyrogenic Reaction

Sudden rise in temperature due to contaminated IV solutions.

Symptoms:
  • High temperature.
  • Chills.
  • Shivering.
Treatment:
  • Stop IV infusion.
  • Give anti-allergy drugs.
  • Apil or Decadron.

2- Air Embolism

Rare complication during central venous catheter insertion.

Prevention:
  • Avoid air entering catheter.
  • Clamp catheter during changing fluids.
  • Use three-way stopcock.
Symptoms:
  • Shock.
  • Cyanosis.
Treatment:
  • Lower head of bed.
  • Patient on left side.
  • Give oxygen.

3- Pulmonary Embolism

Occurs when a clot travels to pulmonary vessels.

Symptoms:
  • Rapid pulse.
  • Rapid breathing.
  • High blood pressure.
  • Cyanosis.
Treatment:
  • ICU admission.
  • Oxygen.
  • Artificial ventilation if needed.
  • Heparin.
  • Streptokinase.

4- Fluid Overload

Occurs after giving large amounts of IV fluids rapidly.

Symptoms:
  • Dyspnea.
  • Cyanosis.
  • Neck vein distension.
  • Hypertension.
  • Cough.
  • Edema.
Treatment:
  • Stop IV fluids.
  • Sitting position (45°).
  • Lasix.

Complications of the Wound

Hematoma

  • Bleeding after surgery.
  • Due to bleeding tendency or surgical error.

Seroma

  • Collection of serous fluid.
  • Occurs after mastectomy or hernia repair.
  • Pressure dressing.
  • Alpha Chymotrypsin.
  • Aspiration if large.

Wound Dehiscence

  • Improper muscle suturing.
  • Occurs after peritonitis or pancreatitis surgery.
  • Serous discharge appears first.
  • Needs urgent exploration.
  • Resuturing.

Respiratory Complications

Atelectasis

  • Bronchial obstruction.
  • Weak breathing.
  • Fever.
  • Rapid pulse.
  • Rapid respiration.
  • Early ambulation.
  • Breathing exercises.
  • Bronchodilators.
  • Expectorants.

Bronchitis

  • Aspiration of gastric contents.
  • High temperature.
  • Dyspnea.
  • Low oxygen.
  • NG tube before emergency surgery.
  • Zantac.
  • Tracheal suction.
  • Hydrocortisone.
  • Antibiotics.

Pneumonia

  • Lung infection.
  • Diagnosed by X-ray or CT.
  • Antibiotics.
  • Rest.
  • Antipyretics.
  • Expectorants.
  • Ventilator if severe.

Cardiac & Venous Complications

Cardiac Arrhythmias

  • Coronary insufficiency.
  • Myocardial infarction.
  • Heart failure.

Deep Vein Thrombosis (DVT)

Causes:
  • Prolonged immobility.
  • Old age.
  • Obesity.
Prevention:
  • LMWH (Clexane).
  • Avoid dehydration.
  • Early ambulation.
Symptoms:
  • Leg swelling.
  • Leg pain.
  • Mild fever.
Diagnosis:
  • Duplex Scan.
Treatment:
  • IV Heparin.
  • Daily PTT.
  • Bed rest.
  • Increase oral fluids.

Pulmonary Embolism

  • Sudden dyspnea.
  • Cyanosis.
  • Rapid weak pulse.
  • Hypotension.
  • ECG changes.
  • Low oxygen.
  • Give oxygen.
  • IV fluids.
  • IV Heparin.
  • IVC Filter if recurrent.

Complications of the Digestive System

1- Paralytic Ileus

  • Occurs due to potassium deficiency.
  • Common after peritonitis and pancreatitis surgery.

2- Gastric Dilatation

  • Occurs after stomach surgery.
  • Causes severe abdominal distension.
  • Difficulty breathing.
  • Treatment: NG Tube + Gastric Suction.

3- Intestinal Obstruction

  • Caused by adhesions or internal hernia.
  • Colicky pain.
  • Abdominal distension.
  • Diagnosed by abdominal X-ray.
  • Treatment: NPO + NG Tube + IV Fluids.

4- Fecal Impaction

  • Common in elderly patients.
  • Diagnosed by Digital Rectal Examination.
  • Treatment: Evacuation.

Liver Complications

  • Hepatitis due to anesthesia allergy.
  • Viral Hepatitis (HBV / HCV).
  • Obstructive Jaundice.

Kidney & Urinary Complications

Urinary Retention

  • Occurs after hernia or prostate surgery.
  • Prevention: Pain relief.
  • Treatment: Analgesics → Relaxation → Catheter if needed.

Urethritis

  • Hospital-acquired infection.
  • Prevent by sterilization.
  • Treatment: Fluids + Antibiotics.

Postoperative Fever

Within 48 Hours

  • Anesthesia.
  • IV Solutions.
  • Blood Transfusion.
  • Atelectasis.
  • Original Disease.

After 48 Hours

  • Wound Infection.
  • Pneumonia.
  • Cannula Infection.
  • UTI.

Day 5 - 7

  • Abscess.
  • Deep Wound Infection.
  • DVT.

Blood Transfusion

Complications

  • Fluid transfusion complications.
  • Incompatibility reaction.
  • Disease transmission.
  • Jaundice.

Symptoms of Incompatibility

  • High Temperature.
  • Rapid Pulse.
  • Hypotension.
  • Kidney Pain.
  • Dyspnea.
  • Cyanosis.

Prevention

  • Correct blood grouping.
  • Correct Cross Match.
  • Verify patient name.
  • Start blood slowly.
  • Observe first 100 ml.

Treatment

  • Stop Blood Immediately.
  • IV Corticosteroids.
  • IV Mannitol.
  • Slow IV Calcium.

Disease Transmission Through Blood

  • HIV.
  • Syphilis.
  • Malaria.
  • Hepatitis B.
  • Hepatitis C.
