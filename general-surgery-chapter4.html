📚 Chapter Four
Postoperative Complications

🏥

Definition

Postoperative complications may result from the patient's disease, the surgery itself, or anesthesia.

📋

Complications of Surgical Operations

  • Complications of anesthesia.
  • Complications of fluid transfusion.
  • Complications of the wound.
  • Respiratory complications.
  • Heart and venous complications.
  • Digestive system complications.
  • Liver complications.
  • Kidney and urinary complications.
  • Fever after surgery.
💉

General Anesthesia Complications

  • Malignant Hyperthermia.
  • Nausea and vomiting.
  • Vocal cord spasm.
  • Urinary retention.
  • Hypothermia.
  • Rare nerve injury.
🩺

Spinal Anesthesia Complications

  • Low blood pressure.
  • Respiratory disturbance.
  • Headache.
  • Urinary retention.
  • Meningitis (Rare).
  • Nerve injury (Rare).
💧

Complications of IV Fluids

Local Complications

  • Fluid leakage around cannula.
  • Phlebitis.
  • Cannula site infection.

General Complications

  • Pyrogenic reaction.
  • Air embolism.
  • Pulmonary embolism.
  • Fluid overload.
🩹

Wound Complications

Hematoma

Collection of blood due to postoperative bleeding.

Seroma

Collection of serous fluid under the skin, commonly after mastectomy or hernia repair.

Wound Dehiscence

Separation of the surgical wound due to poor healing or infection. It requires urgent surgical management.

🫁

Respiratory Complications

  • Atelectasis: Collapse of lung tissue due to bronchial obstruction.
  • Bronchitis: Inflammation caused by aspiration of gastric contents.
  • Pneumonia: Lung infection treated with antibiotics and respiratory support.

Prevention

  • Early mobilization.
  • Breathing exercises.
  • Proper fasting before surgery.
  • Nasogastric tube when indicated.
❤️

Cardiac & Venous Complications

  • Cardiac arrhythmias.
  • Deep Vein Thrombosis (DVT).
  • Pulmonary Embolism.

DVT Prevention

  • LMWH (Clexane).
  • Adequate hydration.
  • Early ambulation.

DVT Symptoms

  • Leg swelling.
  • Leg pain.
  • Mild fever.

Treatment

  • Heparin.
  • Bed rest.
  • Adequate fluids.
🍽️

Digestive System Complications

  • Paralytic Ileus.
  • Gastric Dilatation.
  • Intestinal Obstruction.
  • Fecal Impaction.

Main Treatment

  • NG tube.
  • IV fluids.
  • Stop oral feeding.
  • Surgical intervention if required.
🧫

Liver Complications

  • Hepatitis due to anesthesia allergy.
  • Viral hepatitis after blood transfusion.
  • Obstructive jaundice due to bile duct injury.
🩺

Kidney & Urinary Complications

Urinary Retention

  • Common after hernia or prostate surgery.
  • Managed with analgesics and catheterization if needed.

Urethritis

  • Usually caused by catheter-related infection.
  • Prevented by sterile technique.
  • Treated with fluids and antibiotics.
🌡️

Fever After Surgery

Within 48 Hours

  • Anesthesia.
  • IV Fluids.
  • Blood transfusion.
  • Atelectasis.

After 48 Hours

  • Wound infection.
  • Pneumonia.
  • Urinary tract infection.
  • Cannula infection.

5–7 Days

  • Wound abscess.
  • Internal infection.
  • Deep Vein Thrombosis.
🩸

Blood Transfusion

Main Complications

  • Fluid transfusion complications.
  • Incompatibility reaction.
  • Disease transmission.
  • Jaundice.

Symptoms of Incompatibility

  • Fever and chills.
  • Rapid pulse.
  • Low blood pressure.
  • Difficulty breathing.
  • Cyanosis.

Treatment

  • Stop blood transfusion immediately.
  • IV Corticosteroids.
  • IV Mannitol.
  • Slow IV Calcium.
🧬

Blood Groups

  • Group A → Agglutination with Anti-A.
  • Group B → Agglutination with Anti-B.
  • Group AB → Agglutination with Anti-A & Anti-B.
  • Group O → No agglutination.

Rh Factor

  • Rh Positive (+ve) ≈ 85%.
  • Rh Negative (-ve) ≈ 15%.
  • Important during pregnancy.
