👂 Chapter 9

Diseases of the Nose, Ear and Throat

📖 ملخص الشابتر

👂 Ear Anatomy

  • External Ear → Pinna + Auditory Canal + Tympanic Membrane.
  • Middle Ear → Malleus + Incus + Stapes + Eustachian Tube.
  • Inner Ear → Cochlea + Auditory Nerve.

🔊 Hearing Pathway

  • Sound → Ear Canal → Tympanic Membrane.
  • Ossicles transmit vibration.
  • Cochlea converts vibration into nerve impulses.
  • Auditory Nerve sends signals to Brain.

🟡 Ear Wax (Cerumen)

  • Protects the ear by trapping dust.
  • Do not clean the ear with objects.
  • Removal by doctor if impacted.

🦠 Otitis Externa

  • Diffuse Otitis Externa.
  • Furuncle.
  • Malignant Otitis Externa.
  • Otomycosis.

👂 Otitis Media

  • Acute or Chronic.
  • Symptoms: Ear pain, fever, hearing loss, discharge.
  • Treatment: Antibiotics + Analgesics + Ear Drops.
  • Myringotomy if pus accumulates.

👃 Epistaxis (Nosebleed)

  • Common in summer.
  • First Aid → Lean Forward + Pinch Nose 5–10 min.
  • Never tilt head backward.
  • Severe cases → Packing or Surgery.

🤧 Sinusitis

  • Acute & Chronic.
  • Symptoms: Headache, Fever, Purulent Discharge.
  • Treatment: Antibiotics + Saline + Nasal Spray + Analgesics.

😷 Acute Tonsillitis

  • Usually Streptococcus infection.
  • Symptoms: Sore throat, Fever, Dysphagia.
  • Treatment: Antibiotics (7–10 days), Fluids, Rest.

🔄 Chronic Tonsillitis

  • Repeated infections.
  • Bad breath.
  • Large Tonsils.
  • Treatment → Tonsillectomy.

🩺 Tonsillectomy

  • Indications → Chronic infection, Large tonsils, Peritonsillar Abscess.
  • Contraindications → Bleeding disorders, Severe systemic diseases.
  • Post-op → Recovery Position + Cold Fluids + Soft Diet.

👶 Adenoids

  • Located behind the nose.
  • Cause mouth breathing, snoring and recurrent ear infection.
  • Treatment → Adenoidectomy.

🫁 Laryngeal Obstruction

  • Causes: Allergy, Tumors, Foreign Body, Trauma.
  • Symptoms: Dyspnea, Stridor, Hoarseness.
  • Treatment: Oxygen + Steroids → Tracheostomy if severe.

🩹 Tracheostomy

  • Opening in the trachea to maintain airway.
  • Requires suctioning secretions.
  • Monitor bleeding and breathing.
  • Humidify inspired air.
