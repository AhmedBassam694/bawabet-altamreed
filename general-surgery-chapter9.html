👂 Chapter 9

Diseases of the Nose, Ear and Throat

👂 Anatomy of the Ear

Parts of Ear

  • External Ear → Pinna + Ear Canal + Tympanic Membrane.
  • Middle Ear → Malleus + Incus + Stapes + Eustachian Tube.
  • Inner Ear → Cochlea + Auditory Nerve.

Hearing Pathway

Sound → Pinna → Ear Canal → Eardrum → Ossicles → Cochlea → Auditory Nerve → Brain.

🟠 Ear Wax (Cerumen)

  • Protects ear from dust.
  • Normally comes out naturally.
  • Excess causes hearing loss & blockage.
  • Removed only by doctor.

🦠 Ear Diseases

Otitis Externa

  • Diffuse infection.
  • Furuncle.
  • Malignant (Diabetics).
  • Otomycosis (Fungal).

Treatment: Antibiotics / Antifungals + Cleaning.

Otitis Media

  • Common in children.
  • Occurs after cold or sore throat.
  • Acute or Chronic.
Symptoms
  • Ear pain.
  • Fever.
  • Hearing loss.
  • Pus discharge.
Treatment
  • Antibiotics.
  • Pain killers.
  • Nasal drops.
  • Myringotomy if pus accumulates.
Complications
  • Hearing loss.
  • Damage to ear ossicles.

👃 Nose Disorders

Epistaxis (Nosebleed)

Causes
  • Hypertension.
  • Trauma.
  • Liver disease.
  • Clotting disorders.
  • High temperature.
First Aid
  • Sit leaning forward.
  • Pinch nose 5–10 min.
  • Ice packs.
  • Do NOT tilt head backward.
Treatment
  • Cauterization.
  • Nasal packing.
  • Surgery in severe cases.

😷 Sinusitis

  • Acute or Chronic.
  • Caused by blockage of sinus openings.
Symptoms
  • Headache.
  • Facial pain.
  • Fever.
  • Purulent nasal discharge.
Treatment
  • Antibiotics.
  • Saline wash.
  • Nasal sprays.
  • Pain killers.
  • Surgery for chronic cases.

😮 Tonsillitis

Acute Tonsillitis

Symptoms
  • Sore throat.
  • Difficulty swallowing.
  • High fever.
  • Swollen tonsils.
Treatment
  • Antibiotics (7–10 days).
  • Antipyretics.
  • Fluids.
  • Rest.

Chronic Tonsillitis

  • Repeated infection.
  • Bad breath.
  • Snoring.
  • Difficulty swallowing.

Treatment: Tonsillectomy.

🏥 Tonsillectomy

Indications

  • Chronic infection.
  • Peritonsillar abscess.
  • Large tonsils.
  • Sleep apnea.
  • Suspicion of tumor.

Post-operative Care

  • Recovery position.
  • Watch for bleeding.
  • Cold fluids after 6 hrs.
  • Soft food.
  • Avoid hot & spicy food.

👶 Adenoids

  • Behind the nose.
  • Cause mouth breathing.
  • Snoring.
  • Repeated ear infections.
  • Treatment → Adenoidectomy.

😴 Snoring

Causes
  • Obesity.
  • Deviated septum.
  • Large tonsils.
  • Nasal polyps.
Treatment
  • Weight loss.
  • Surgery if obstruction exists.
  • CPAP in some patients.

🫁 Laryngeal Obstruction

Causes
  • Edema.
  • Tumors.
  • Foreign body.
  • Trauma.
Symptoms
  • Difficulty breathing.
  • Stridor.
  • Hoarseness.
Treatment
  • Oxygen.
  • Steroids.
  • Tracheostomy if severe.

🩺 Tracheostomy

Indications
  • Laryngeal obstruction.
  • Coma with secretions.
  • Before some surgeries.
Nursing Care
  • Semi-sitting position.
  • Suction secretions.
  • Clean tube.
  • Monitor bleeding & fever.
  • Humidify air.
